# Tooliva — Brand & ASO

## Working brand

# TOOLIVA

Working descriptor:

**Tooliva — Cleaner, App Lock & Tools**

The brand is intentionally broader than “Cleaner” so future modules fit naturally.

Before paid marketing, perform a fresh trademark/store/domain check. This document is product branding, not trademark clearance.

## Positioning

### Main line

**All your Android tools. One clean app.**

### Trust line

**No fake boost. No fake cooling. Just useful tools.**

### Privacy line

**Your files stay on your phone.**

## Google Play title hypotheses

- `Tooliva: Cleaner & App Lock`
- `Tooliva: Android Tools`
- `Tooliva: Cleaner & Utilities`

Do not keyword-stuff the title.

## Short description hypotheses

- `Clean storage, protect apps, check your phone and use everyday tools in one app.`
- `Cleaner, privacy tools, phone diagnostics, QR, PDF, images and more.`
- `A useful Android toolbox for storage, privacy, files, diagnostics and quick tools.`

## Screenshot story

## Icon direction

Avoid:
- broom
- rocket
- fake speedometer/boost
- virus cliché
- Android robot

Direction:
- geometric `T`
- modular tiles/four segments
- negative-space toolbox concept
- clean adaptive icon
- recognizable at small size

## Visual direction

- graphite surfaces
- one bright functional accent
- large numeric data
- rounded Material 3 cards
- restrained motion
- minimal alarm-red usage

Tooliva should feel closer to a premium system utility than a cheap cleaner.
