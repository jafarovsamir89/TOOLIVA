# Tooliva — Brand & Google Play Positioning

Revision: 2026-08-16

## Working brand

# TOOLIVA

Primary descriptor:

**Cleaner, File Manager & Device Tools**

The brand should not be reduced to `AI Cleaner`. Tooliva's differentiator is not a vague AI label; it is a more trustworthy and complete device-maintenance product.

Before paid marketing, perform trademark/domain/social-handle checks in target markets.

---

# Positioning

## Main promise

**Find what takes your storage. Clean it safely. Manage your Android.**

## Trust promise

**No fake boost. No fake cooling. Every cleanup explained.**

## Privacy promise

**Your storage scan stays on your device.**

---

# Store-title hypotheses

Use the final current Play title constraints when publishing; do not keyword-stuff.

Candidates:
- `Tooliva: Cleaner & File Manager`
- `Tooliva: Storage Cleaner`
- `Tooliva: Cleaner & Files`

The store description must prominently promote real file-management/on-device-search functionality if the production build requests All Files Access.

---

# Short-description hypotheses

- `Find large files, clean storage safely, manage folders and check your Android.`
- `Deep storage cleaner, file manager, duplicates, screenshots and device tools.`
- `See what uses your storage, remove what you choose and manage your files.`

Never claim a feature before it is present in the production build.

# Visual identity

Keep existing `docs/design/` direction:
- dark graphite
- bright teal/cyan primary action
- premium system-tool feel
- large numeric storage information
- rounded Material 3 surfaces
- restrained secondary colors
- no aggressive red panic UI

The interface should feel more like a premium Android system utility than a cheap ad-driven cleaner.

---

# Icon direction

Avoid category clichés:
- broom only
- rocket boost
- fake speedometer
- virus shield

Preferred:
- geometric Tooliva `T`
- modular/storage-inspired shape
- adaptive icon
- strong silhouette at small size

Cleaner/file-manager capability is communicated by listing/screenshots, not by turning the logo into a broom.

---

# Tone of voice

Preferred:
- `Files larger than 1 GB`
- `8 old APK installers`
- `Review 824 MB of screenshots`
- `Moved 2.4 GB to Trash`
- `620 MB physically freed`
- `Full Storage Mode is off — results are limited`

Avoid:
- `DANGER!`
- `Your phone is 87% damaged`
- `Boost now!`
- `Critical junk detected!`
- `Virus risk!` unless a legitimate security engine exists

---

# Competitive creative angle

The market is already full of `one tap cleaner` claims. Tooliva should advertise **understanding and control**:

- `Where did your 128 GB go?`
- `Find the folder eating your storage.`
- `Old APKs, ZIPs, PDFs and videos — all in one scan.`
- `A cleaner that tells you what it actually removed.`
- `Cleaner + File Manager, without fake boosting.`
